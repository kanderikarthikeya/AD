Motion Capture Activity - College Guide
1

Start Live Motion Capture

Click Start Capture in the top header. Allow webcam access when prompted by the browser. Stand 1.5 to 2.5 meters away so your upper body or full body is visible.

2

Explore 3D Digital Character Modes

Switch between the 3D Robot Mannequin, Cyber Skeleton, and Neon Wire avatars. Use your mouse or touchscreen to orbit, pan, and inspect the character from any camera angle.

3

Record & Playback Motion Keyframes

Click Record to capture a sequence of movements. Click Stop Rec when finished. Use the playback timeline scrubber, loop toggle, and speed selector to inspect the recorded motion in slow-motion.

How It Works (For College Viva & Demos):
1. Computer Vision & ML: Uses Google MediaPipe BlazePose to infer 33 3D body keypoints directly on the client side using WebAssembly and WebGL acceleration.
2. Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Filtering: Raw camera landmark coordinates undergo mathematical low-pass smoothing to eliminate high-frequency video jitter.
3. Forward Kinematics & Three.js 3D Rigging: The 3D coordinates drive a volumetric geometric humanoid avatar with articulated limb segments and joint rotations.
4. Zero Server Overhead: Runs 100% locally in your browser with private client-side processing.
